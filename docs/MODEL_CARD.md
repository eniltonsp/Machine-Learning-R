# Model card

## Task and intended use

Binary classification of **declared return intent after complaint service**. The service status, customer rating and completion duration are retrospective predictors. They must not be represented as features available at complaint opening. This is not a prediction of observed purchase behaviour or a causal model of service interventions.

## Current availability

No validated model is bundled. The historical HTML reports 12,646 observations and holdout accuracy 0.900 / ROC AUC 0.951 for an older feature set. These numbers are historical, not rerun or claimed for this implementation. The latest complaint CSV has 11,699 records across 14 companies; a prepared TXT has 8,574 records across nine companies. Both lack the individual return-intent target, rating and complete response/completion timing fields. The synthetic demo intentionally has no target and cannot be used to substantiate model quality.

## Training and evaluation

`scripts/train.R` validates schema, numeric ranges, target completeness, duplicate rows and optional complaint IDs. A minimum of 200 rows and 50 examples per class is a technical guard, not a statistical adequacy claim.

With dates, the most recent distinct dates are held out, without splitting a date across partitions. Without dates, a stratified random holdout is used and explicitly labelled. Five-fold stratified cross-validation within training compares logistic regression to eight XGBoost configurations. Preprocessing is fitted within each fold. AUC explicitly treats Yes as the event. Selection uses cross-validation, not holdout performance.

The holdout reports accuracy, AUC, sensitivity and specificity for Yes, Brier score, confusion counts and a training-majority baseline. The final deployed workflow is refitted on all labelled rows after evaluation. Input checksum, seed, split strategy and session/package versions are recorded locally. Keep the evaluated source version with the reports.

## Limitations and release criteria

- No present claim of predictive improvement, calibration, fairness or business impact.
- Sampling of online complainants does not represent all customers.
- Random inner folds are not rolling temporal validation; temporal robustness needs additional evaluation.
- Exact duplicate checks do not identify paraphrases or repeated consumers; review leakage by source IDs and collection history.
- Before deployment, assess subgroup errors, probability calibration, drift, and whether a simple baseline performs comparably.
- Predicted P(Yes) is a model estimate, not a guaranteed or calibrated probability.
- Batch scoring requires the trusted server-side model generated by this repository. User-uploaded RDS files are never accepted.
- No SHAP explanations are claimed. Word frequency is not model importance or sentiment.
